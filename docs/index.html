The problem

Two displays. One identity.
That's not minimalism. That's a coin flip.

Every same-model panel ships the same placeholder serial in its identity record. macOS reads it, shrugs, and re-deals your desk on every reconnect. You're not arranging it wrong. It's not you. It's your EDID.

The breakthrough

Introducing the Serial Engine.

Deep in IOKit, every panel still whispers its true factory serial. The one on the sticker. The one macOS heroically ignores. docksteady chains it through the display pipe to the window server: a firm link between glass and identity. We call it grounding. Because it grounds things.

Features. So many features.

Our most complete docksteady ever.

Steady Modes

Lid open. Clamshell. Each arrangement remembered. Seamlessly? No. Separately. Which is better.

Dynamic Poll

Checks every 45 seconds. All day. Every day. It never sleeps, except when your Mac does. It's a while loop. A pro while loop.

Geometry Replay

Your exact arrangement, replayed to the pixel. Any offset. Any seam. Industry-leading sameness.

Swap Windows. Boom.

When macOS trades your windows, one click sweeps them home. Full screen included. Split View comes back as two Spaces. That's growth.

Privacy. Obviously.

No network. No telemetry. No account. It doesn't even want to know your name. It wants to know your serial. The monitor's.

It Stands Down

Arranging displays yourself? docksteady waits, respectfully. A good butler, with a launchd contract.
